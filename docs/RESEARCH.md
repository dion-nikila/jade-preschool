# Jade: research and creative rationale

Researched 27 October 2026. This is an independent proposal for Jade Preschool and Daycare in Colombo, not an official school publication.

## The commercial objective

Help a parent decide whether a visit is worthwhile, then make starting that conversation easy. A preschool purchase is both emotional and practical: a parent needs to picture their child belonging and understand whether the programme fits their family. A visually memorable homepage is the beginning of that journey; the programme, approach, daily-life and admissions pages need to hold up afterwards.

The following decisions are design judgments informed by the sources below, not measured conversion results. No uplift, conversion-rate or enrolment claim is being made.

## What was researched

| Source | Observation | Decision for Jade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Jade’s current homepage](https://jadepreschool.com/) | Real school photography, public telephone and an Asoka Gardens address are available. The existing presentation repeats its hero message. | Use school-specific photographs, concise original copy and a coherent visual system. Keep contact details easy to find. |
| [Jade: About Us](https://jadepreschool.com/about-us) | Public facilities and leadership information exist, but repeated subheadings reduce clarity. | Explain the approach in three substantive ideas. Avoid reproducing a long facilities checklist or asserting unverified credentials. |
| [Jade: Admission](https://jadepreschool.com/admission/) | Playgroup, Nursery, LKG, UKG, Day Care and after-school care are described. Day Care lists a 20-month minimum. This page contains a different address from the homepage. | Create four clear programme sections, with LKG and UKG grouped under Kindergarten. Carry programme choice into the enquiry. Flag location uncertainty rather than silently ignoring it. |
| [Jade International School’s public LinkedIn page](https://lk.linkedin.com/school/jade-international-school/) | Recent preschool promotion names Asoka Gardens and the same telephone number as the school homepage. | Prefer Asoka Gardens for the prototype; ask the school to confirm it before an official launch. Do not hard-code an intake campaign that will expire. |
| [The Aspen Grove School](https://www.aspengrovephilly.com/) | Learning spaces, nature, age-group information and the school’s point of view make the offer concrete. | Give Jade an approach page and a programme destination; let photography carry some of the explanation. Do not imply Jade follows Aspen Grove’s pedagogy. |
| [Guidepost: Nido](https://guidepostmontessori.au/nido/) | A programme page connects materials and daily experiences to a particular developmental stage. | Describe what children actually do in each programme, rather than repeating the same promise four times. Do not borrow Montessori terminology for Jade. |
| [Creative Play Corner: Step Inside](https://creativeplaycorner.com/step-inside/) | The visit journey is approachable and answers parent questions in ordinary language. | Use a low-pressure invitation, a short enquiry and clear next steps. A message is not a confirmed appointment. |
| [NAEYC: What Does a High-Quality Preschool Program Look Like?](https://www.naeyc.org/our-work/families/what-does-high-quality-program-for-preschool-look-like) | Relationships, meaningful activities, individual development and family communication are useful lenses for evaluating early-years provision. | Discuss learning through tangible experiences and family conversations. Never suggest NAEYC accreditation. |
| [NAEYC: Support Learning With Play](https://www.naeyc.org/our-work/families/support-learning-with-play) | Engaged play can support curiosity, problem solving, attention and persistence. | Show examples such as conversations through stories, early counting through games, and taking turns with materials. Keep educational claims proportionate. |
| [ChildCare.gov: Look, Listen, and Ask](https://childcare.gov/consumer-education/find-and-choose-quality-child-care/choosing-quality-child-care-tip-sheets) | Visiting allows families to observe spaces, routines and teacher interactions. | Make the school visit the primary action and provide daily-life imagery before asking for contact. This is parent-decision guidance, not Sri Lankan regulation. |
| [W3C WAI: Carousel Pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/) | Automatically changing slides introduce control, focus and reading-order considerations. | Use a still photographic layout and a user-opened native dialog instead of an autoplay carousel. |
| [Reggio Children: Values](https://www.reggiochildren.it/en/reggio-emilia-approach/valori-en/) | The relationship between environment, expression and learning provides a useful reference for early-years storytelling. | Give children’s work and interactions visual space. Do not present Jade as a Reggio school. |

## Visual direction

The school and programme pages use a prospectus-like structure with Jade’s original logo, school photography, Newsreader display typography and DM Sans for practical information. Following the requested playful direction, the homepage combines a sunny background, layered photo frames, independently fluttering butterflies and botanical SVG flowers that bloom with scrolling. The existing logo colours inform the artwork. A pause control and reduced-motion support are part of that motion design.

The homepage layers a photograph of a teacher and children at the painting table with a photograph of the outdoor play space. Programmes are presented as a readable index rather than repeated promotional cards. Inner pages cover the approach, facilities and verified leadership names. Captions describe the photographs plainly. The gallery is a photographic record, with a user-opened photo viewer.

Copy uses ordinary school language. Programme names, learning activities, location, contact information and admissions questions carry the explanation. The school’s logo is retained. The butterflies and planting are decorative illustrations, not a new school mascot. The photographs remain real school images.

Additional visual reference: [Pentagram’s education work](https://www.pentagram.com/education), including [The New School identity](https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-new-school/story), informed the principle that a school should have a coherent institution-specific identity. [St Christopher School](https://www.stchris.co.uk/) was visually reviewed as an independent-school reference. The prototype does not copy these organisations’ branding or claim affiliation.

## Information architecture

| Page | Parent’s question | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Does this feel like somewhere my child could belong? | Explore programmes or arrange a visit. |
| Our approach | What does learning here mean in practice? | Understand the philosophy and talk to the school. |
| Programmes | What would be appropriate for my child? | Open an enquiry with that programme selected. |
| Life at Jade | What might my child experience here? | Inspect the school photographs. |
| Visit & admissions | How do we begin, and what should we ask? | Prepare, review and send a WhatsApp enquiry. |
| Privacy & credits | Is this official, and what happens to my details? | Read a direct explanation of the prototype and its services. |

## Editorial guardrails

- No fabricated parent testimonials, star ratings, awards, outcome statistics or staff credentials.
- No invented prices, availability, visit slots or operational hours.
- No claim that a submitted form books an appointment.
- No scroll instructions, interaction tutorials, fake live counters, autoplay media or decorative loading screens.
- No synthetic school photographs or portraits presented as real people.
- Everyday activities are described using the school’s published programme; no fictional timetable is presented.
- Photographs remain the property of their original owners. Existing public publication is not a substitute for the school confirming permission for an official relaunch.

## A real launch would need

Confirm the current campus address, programme age cutoffs, complete fees, sessions and care hours. Obtain school approval of copy, proposed branding and image permissions. Add verified staff information and a genuine family testimonial only if supplied and approved. Confirm how admissions enquiries will be handled. If the school later needs a direct form backend, specify delivery, consent, retention and spam controls before replacing the current reviewable WhatsApp flow.

The public concept is marked as independent and carries `noindex,follow` so it is not intended to compete with the official school website in search. Remove that setting only when the school approves an official deployment. At that point, set the final domain, canonical URLs, social image and verified school structured data.
